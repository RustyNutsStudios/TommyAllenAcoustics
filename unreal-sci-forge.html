Sci Forge

SCI-FI WEAPON SOUND DESIGNER FOR UNREAL

Build a plasma bolt, a railgun, a beam or a cryo weapon inside the Unreal Editor — charge-up, discharge, cooldown, dry fire — then press one button and it writes ready-to-use SoundCue and MetaSound assets into your open project. Pure synthesis, 44 layers, sitting on real blast physics.

>> WHAT IT DOES

Open the Sci Forge tab, design a weapon, press SEND TO UNREAL. SoundWaves, a plain SoundCue that plays a random take per trigger, and a MetaSound built with Epic's own MetaSound Builder — one folder, named after your weapon.

The button follows the view: SIMPLE exports one shot, DETAILED a whole weapon, DUAL FADE two voices that crossfade as the cell drains.

>> WHAT YOU GET

  • The ENERGY stage — CHARGE, SUCK, VACUUM, DUCK, COOLDOWN, BEAM, ARC, GOO, ICE. Nine layers no conventional gun has, over real blast physics so it has mass instead of sounding like a synth patch.
  • The Weapon Bench — FIRST, NORMAL, LAST and EMPTY, with a cell that counts down.
  • Ammo-state export — one cue per slot plus a MetaSound that routes a single trigger from the count.
  • Dual fade — a rifle that goes from clean to unstable as the cell drains. Anything you can normalise to 0-1 drives it: heat, charge, damage.
  • 44 layers, 223 parameters — across TRANSIENT, ENERGY, SUBS, MECHS, VERB and MIX.
  • Variation sets — 5, 10 or 15 distinct takes per import.
  • Royalty free — every sound you generate is yours, any project, no attribution.

>> THE TECHNICAL BIT

Editor-only. It adds nothing to your packaged game. The sounds it builds are ordinary Unreal assets and ship like any other asset.

Unreal Engine 5.6, 5.7 and 5.8, Windows editor. Each engine version has its own build.

Requires two plugins that ship with Unreal — Python Editor Script Plugin (runs the import) and MetaSound (the asset type it generates). Both are enabled automatically. Nothing third-party to download.

Assets build under /Game/TAA/SciForge/YourName/.
